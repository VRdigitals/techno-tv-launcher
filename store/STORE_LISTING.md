**Full description** (4000 max)
```
Make your TV feel like the future. Techno TV replaces the cluttered, ad-filled home screen with a clean, cinematic dashboard, and a talking assistant called NOVA.

NO ADS. NO CLUTTER.
• No "recommended for you" rows or sponsored banners. Just your apps, your inputs and what matters.

A HOME SCREEN THAT TALKS BACK
• NOVA reacts to what you pick: rest on an app and it has something to say.
• Switch to HDMI and it comments ("Going for gaming, huh?").
• Leave the TV idle and NOVA suggests something to watch; press OK to open it.
• Hold OK on NOVA's card to mute it any time.

CINEMATIC START-UP
• When you turn the TV on, an arc-reactor style start-up screen powers up and NOVA welcomes you back, then you're straight into your home screen.
• Bluetooth speaker? Techno TV waits for it to reconnect, so you never miss the welcome.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Clock, date and local weather (found automatically).
• Featured apps as big cards, plus an All apps page.
• Inputs panel: TV, HDMI, AV and USB in one click.
• Quick settings: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, picture and sound.
• Cast & Connect shortcuts: screen mirroring, Bluetooth headphones, devices.

SMOOTH ON TV HARDWARE
• A 3D blue glow follows your remote. Animations are tuned to stay smooth on everyday TVs.

PRIVATE
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Nothing about you leaves your TV (see our privacy policy).

ABOUT THE HOME BUTTON
Google TV doesn't let apps replace its home screen. If you agree, Techno TV uses an Android accessibility service only to open itself when you press Home and to show the start-up screen when the TV turns on. It doesn't read your screen or collect data, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Accessibility.

Works on Google TV and Android TV running Android 12 or newer.
```
