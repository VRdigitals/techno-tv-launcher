# Techno TV: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Techno TV is a home screen (launcher) for Google TV and Android TV, made by VR Digitals.
It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What the app uses

- **Approximate location, for the weather card.** If you haven't set a city, the app asks
  [ipapi.co](https://ipapi.co/privacy/) once a week for the approximate city of your internet
  connection (based on your IP address), then asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)
  for that city's weather. Only the city and coordinates are stored, on the TV.
- **The list of apps installed on your TV**, to show them on the home screen. It never leaves
  the TV.
- **The TV's text-to-speech engine**, to speak assistant lines. Speech is generated on the TV.
- **An accessibility service** (only if you turn it on). It notices when the Google TV home
  screen opens, so the Home button can open Techno TV instead, and when the screen turns on, to
  show the start-up screen. It does not read what is on your screen and collects no data.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not use advertising IDs, analytics or crash-reporting services.
- It does not record audio or use the microphone.

## Children

Techno TV is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children.
